# Content rules (Care Setu)

- **Register first, write second.** A sentence that states a fact (number, certification,
  compliance, partner, "trusted by", response time, availability, language, price, contact detail,
  domain) needs a `docs/CLAIMS.md` row **before** it is written. Only `approved` or `sourced` rows ship (D-007).
- **Never ship:** hospital names/logos without a signed partnership; ISO/HIPAA/CEA/"DPDP
  compliant" claims; "trusted by thousands"; the placeholder `+91 123 456 7890`; any domain or
  email not confirmed owned (Q2); AI-generated people shown as real staff.
- **Services:** only lines marked `live` (Q6) are presented as available; others are "coming
  soon" or hidden. Never imply medicine sales, teleconsultation, vaccination or EMI before Q6 clears
  them (COMPLIANCE.md).
- **Legal pages** (`/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refunds`) show a visible "DRAFT, not legal advice,
  pending professional review" marker until a lawyer/CA sign-off D-entry exists (D-012).
- **Voice:** plain, warm, specific; short sentences for anxious readers; no medical promises
  ("guaranteed recovery"), no fear-selling.
- **Translations:** human-written or human-reviewed only (D-008).
- **Owner copy approval:** new or changed page copy is shown to Sasanka for founder approval (Q15)
  before it goes live.
