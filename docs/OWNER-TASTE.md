# Owner taste log

**Purpose.** What the owner agreed and disagreed with, round by round, and what it says about the design he wants.
Read this before planning any page, and use the "Current read" to aim the sample hunt (tool list, Mobbin, refs)
at his taste. Decisions still live in `DECISIONS.md`; this file is the evidence behind them.

**How a round is recorded.** Date · what was shown · verbatim (or near) reactions · likes · dislikes · references
he gave · then update "Current read" and "Aim the next sample hunt". Separate what he *said* from what we *infer*.

---

## Current read (after round 1)
- **Wants:** premium, cinematic, emotional; real footage or motion carrying feeling before information; big
  confident type; bold brand moments (the moving wordmark).
- **Doesn't want:** light background + simple blue buttons, the "normal Indian healthcare website" look;
  flat catalogue pages.
- **Motion:** likes motion that tells a story (the ward-to-door trip) and ambient brand motion (footer). Wants
  more of it (asked for animation on Services).
- **Unknowns to test in round 2:** dark vs warm-light pages; how warm (brown/sand like the reference?); how much
  real-life imagery vs graphic motifs; whether the metro-line motif survives in the new look.

## Aim the next sample hunt
Editorial / cinematic wellness and premium-care sites with video heroes, warm neutral palettes, large grotesk type,
pill CTAs; scroll-told stories. Avoid: Indian clinic templates, blue-on-white SaaS health.
